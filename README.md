Modified Steam Rankine Cycle: Reheat & Regeneration Analysis
Advanced thermodynamic modeling and performance optimization of a modified steam power plant cycle incorporating both reheat and regenerative feedwater heating systems.
Key Objectives
Modified Cycle Analysis: Evaluate how structural modifications (intermediate steam reheating and multiple feedwater stages) alter standard Rankine cycle behavior.
Efficiency Optimization: Assess the impact of reheat pressures and extraction steam fractions on thermal efficiency and turbine work output.
Mass & Energy Balances: Perform comprehensive first and second-law thermodynamic analyses.
Tools & Contents
Tools: Engineering Equation Solver (EES) / Thermodynamic Property Relations.
Files Included: Detailed calculation report, T-s diagrams, and complete performance analysis
